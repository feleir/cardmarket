# Privacy policy: Wants Optimizer for Cardmarket

This extension only runs on `www.cardmarket.com`.

**What it reads:** your Cardmarket wants lists, the offer pages of the cards on them, sellers' offer pages filtered by your wants list, and your shopping cart page. It reads them in your own browser, with your own login, the same way you would by opening those pages.

**What it stores:** your settings, and the offer pages it read in the last 30 minutes, kept in your browser's storage for cardmarket.com. Nothing else.

**What it sends:** nothing to the developer or anyone else. The only requests it makes go to cardmarket.com: reading pages, and adding the articles you choose to your cart when you press the button. There is no analytics, no tracking and no remote code. Everything the extension runs, including the optional exact solver, ships inside the extension.
